Published specs, corrected for reality

Nameplate numbers are optimistic. A rack is rated for 600 kW, a drive is rated for five years, an interconnection queue is rated in gigawatts. None of those survive contact with a real site. These are free tools that do the correction, on public data, with every constant shown.

The bench

Rack Budget

Power, cooling and floor area for an AI hall. Start from a megawatt budget or from a fleet size and get the other side, plus the heat you have to reject and the room it lands in.

How these are built

01

Public data only

Every input is something you could go and download yourself. Nothing proprietary, nothing behind an NDA, nothing you have to take on trust.

02

Constants in the open

Each tool states its assumptions and shows the arithmetic that produced every number. If you disagree with a constant, you know exactly which one to argue with.

03

One decision out

Numbers on a screen are data. Each tool ends by naming the thing that will actually stop you, because that is the part someone has to act on.
