SOURCES & METHODOLOGY

One incident. Two timelines.

The customer timeline begins at the first impact recorded in the provider’s retrospective. The public timeline begins at the first acknowledgement recorded in the original InfraBench dataset.

Reading the replay

The clock reveals recorded events as they happen. The overview shows the full historical duration, so you can see where you are. Event text is a condensed reconstruction, not a live feed or a complete verbatim archive.

Your hypothetical alert

Alert time = first impact + your selected delay. The comparison assumes your monitoring detects this incident after that delay; it does not predict real monitoring performance.

Evidence matters

AWS December 2021 and Azure October 2025 use reported acknowledgement times. Azure’s time is a proxy and the first advisory may have been earlier. Other records are marked primary in the original dataset. Sources are linked per incident.

Comparing the six

Gap = first public acknowledgement − first customer impact. Your lead = gap − alert delay. A lead greater than five minutes is “You first”; within five minutes either way is “Too close”; below minus five minutes is “Provider first”. Total duration runs from first impact to the final all-clear.

Historical records carried over from InfraBench’s original Outage Replay. No synthetic error-rate or traffic measurements are added.
